# Cherry Auto Matchmaker

**Automotive sales lane** — vehicle research packets, comps, buyer matching, listing assets, and follow-up sequences.

## 1. What this project does
Produces complete vehicle sales packets: market position, pricing range, photo plan, listing, ideal buyer, objection responses, and follow-up sequence.

## 2. Who it is for
Cherry, dealership-support roles, and portfolio reviewers.

## 3. What exists today
Foundation structure.

## 4. What is planned
One complete vehicle sales packet as the first proof.

## 5. What it does not claim
Does not broker vehicles, collect funds, or represent third-party sellers. Research and support framing only. Licensing considerations are respected.

## 6. First proof artifact
One complete vehicle sales packet (market comps, ideal buyer, listing assets, objections, follow-up sequence).
